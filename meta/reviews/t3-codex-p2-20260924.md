## Limits

- T3 is not a byte replication of T2. Every Codex arm carries Codex's own context floor (M91, M93),
  which is identical within a rung. The comparisons are within Codex, and the floor could damp an
  addition's effect equally in every arm.
- The tool list sent to the model is not observable locally (M91). No run recorded a tool call.
- Blinding leaks the arm category (guided or unguided), as in T2. It does not leak the clause or
  the examples.
- Three repeats of 7 pairs are enough to detect a large effect, not a small one. The result is no
  detected benefit at the pre-registered margin, not evidence of equivalence.
