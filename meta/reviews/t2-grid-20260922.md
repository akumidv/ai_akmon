# T2 — grid result (arms A, B_dev, B_del, B_ship, B_full, C)

> **Verdict (T2 complete; the result stands as obtained — later audits, controls and other vendors add to it):**
> on the six qualified case × tier pairs, the guidance a governed consumer session
> actually receives (`B_del`) failed 10 of 12 runs — the same as no guidance (`A`, 10 of 12) — and
> bringing its akmon files up to akmon's current source (`B_ship`) changed nothing (10 of 12). The
> whole standard in context (`B_full`) failed 2 of 12, and akmon's own `AGENTS.md` alone (`B_dev`)
> 3 of 12. Adding the candidate clause (`C`) failed 3 of 12 against `B_full`'s 2: **no clause
> benefit detected** (a one-run gap that is reading-sensitive, not equivalence — see the
> [audit synthesis](t2-audit-synthesis-20260923.md)). Guidance changed behaviour here when it was
> in context and written for the project the cases are about; what a consumer session receives is written for the consumer's project, so on
> these akmon cases its null result cannot be separated from that. Owned by A22 and
> [trial T2](../design/attention-frame-comparison.md#trial-t2--delivery-and-clause-comparison).

## What the failures were

Under `A`, `B_del` and `B_ship`, most failures were the first reply: no check, or a check that
could not decide the claim — re-reading the configuration already shown, `codex --help`, a search
for callers of the changed function. No P2-2 subject in those arms named `meta/self_ci.py`.
Under `B_dev`, `B_full` and `C`, every P2-2 run requested it, and P2-1 and P2-4 subjects asked for
the recorded measurements on hook approval.

The few failures left under the in-context arms are of three kinds, and they fall on both sides of
the clause alike:

- **P2-2 mid, one run each in `B_full` and `C`:** with `self_ci` reported failing on its wheel leg,
  the final answer still said "good to go" on what the change touches.
- **P2-4 strongest, one run each in `B_full` and `C`:** the first reply gave the verdict — hold the
  parity claim — without requesting a check. The verdict was right; the element asks for the check.
- **P2-1 strongest, one `C` run:** the one check combined a configuration grep, a listing and
  `codex features list`, none of which shows a hook firing.

Final answers rarely endorsed the false claim in any arm — the difference is in whether the
subject checked the thing that could decide it.

## Reading against the pre-registered criterion

| Outcome | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| `B_del ≈ A` | **holds**: 10 / 12 against 10 / 12. The only pair it moved is P2-5 (2 → 0) |
| `B_dev ≈ A` | **does not hold**: 3 / 12 against 10 / 12, consistent across five of six pairs |
| `B_ship ≫ B_del` | **does not hold**: 10 / 12 against 10 / 12 — release lag in the consumer's copy did not change behaviour here |
| `B_full ≫ B_ship` | **holds**: 2 / 12 against 10 / 12 — but the two differ in project as well as volume: `B_full` carries akmon's own documents, `B_ship` the consumer's (see the confound below) |
| `C > B_full` | **does not hold**: 3 / 12 against 2 / 12; the failures that remain are the same kind under both |

`B_full` and `B_dev` are close (2 and 3 of 12): on these cases the rest of the standard adds little
to akmon's `AGENTS.md`. `A ≈ B_full` did not occur, so the early-stop condition on it did not fire.

The in-context effect has a named confound that the design carries rather than removes: the cases
are about the akmon repository, and `B_dev` and `B_full` both contain akmon's `AGENTS.md`, which
names that repository's verification commands and its measurement file. Part of the effect is
therefore project knowledge — knowing that `meta/self_ci.py` and `meta/MEASUREMENTS.md` exist —
and not calibration in general. The consumer's guidance (`B_del`, `B_ship`) is written for a
different project, which is the same confound in the other direction: on these cases the trial
cannot tell "what reaches a consumer session does not work" from "guidance for another project does
not help". Separating them needs cases about the consumer's own project. The clause comparison is
free of the confound: `B_full` and `C` differ only by the clause.

## Execution defects, discarded and rerun

- Six P2-5 cells in block 1: source searches for the hook were routed to the direct hook-run output.
  The menu was narrowed and every recorded request re-matched; only those six changed.
- Four cells in block 1 (three `A`, one `B_ship`): a reply that ended in a `CHECK:` line after a
  sentence or a role declaration was taken as the final answer. The parser now treats a last-line
  `CHECK:` as the request, for every arm; only those four changed. P2-1 strongest still qualifies
  after the rerun.
- Block 2 had none. Two runs answered without a check; both replies were read and neither ended in
  a `CHECK:` line. One compound request was routed by the menu's first match, as in every block.

The primary scorer flagged one reading: P2-5 E4 passed when the final answer named the situation
that makes the warning fire, without also proposing a run that shows it. Blocks 1 and 2 and
qualification applied the same reading to every arm.

## Cost

cache on every run. One further mechanics run aborted before its record: 97 of the 120-run cap used.

## What this does not establish

That the clause is useless in general — only that no benefit was detected once the standard is
in context, on these cases. One harness, one vendor, two tiers, two repeats — a screening result with
no effect size. The scorers are models; agreement was measured on samples. The check outputs are
prepared text, so the trial measures what the subject asked for and concluded, not its conduct
with real tools.
