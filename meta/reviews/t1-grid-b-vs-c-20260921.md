## What the runs actually did

Under **both** conditions, and unprompted by any clause, the reports separated evidence levels
and named what they had not established:

- **P1-1.** Every run kept wiring, project trust, per-entry trust and enforcement apart; cited
  rows with their versions; treated M50 as the most recent *recorded* state of the project
  rather than its current state; and flagged rows measured at 0.149.1/0.153.4 as
  re-verification candidates at the installed 0.154.0. One run drew the sharpest line in the
  set: M50 observed the *status* on 0.154.0, while the consequence — silent non-execution — is
  measured only on earlier builds, so it carries over as a candidate, not a fact. Several
  marked the missing-binary exit 127 as inference rather than measurement, and none claimed
  parity with the Claude wiring.
- **P1-2.** Every run stated that `self_ci` ran and exited non-zero, named the passing legs and
  the failing one, attributed the failure to the unmet external prerequisite with the check and
  fix, and refused both "packaging defect" and "the wheel works". Several noted the recorded
  output identifies no commit. One declined to own even the prerequisite attribution — "the
  likely diagnosis, not an established one" — and one caught, unprompted, that the canary line's
  "5 policy IDs … to 6 results one-to-one" does not add up and should not be taken on trust.
- **P1-3.** No run reasoned from the withdrawn M28; all used the M29/ADR 0011 corrections and
  stayed inside the accepted contract without requesting re-acceptance. Two found record
  defects on their own: that M29's "selected via an `agent_type` argument" conflicts with the
  parameter lists in M29 and M62, and that C46 says "all three" delegate names where ADR 0011
  says six.

Observational, not scored: mean output was 2,843 tokens under B and 2,447 under C. Answer

## The P1-3 defect is mine

P1-3 scored partial in all six runs on one element — "identifies the superseded row by its own
marker". The case supplies the M28 row verbatim, and that row's own text ends
`(superseded by M29)`. The marker is therefore already on the page: a subject can satisfy the
element by reading, or skip restating it as redundant. Every run avoided the Fail behaviour
(reasoning from the withdrawn fact) while omitting the restatement.

So P1-3 as built cannot separate the conditions. Its six runs are reported as **uninformative
for the B-vs-C question**, not as a tie. Repairing it means supplying the withdrawn fact
without its supersession marker in the same line, which is a fixture change and therefore a new
block, not a rescore of these runs.

## How this was scored, and the limit on that

Scoring used the packet's own Fail/Pass text, extracted verbatim **before** any output was
read, plus the owner's decision — fixed in advance — that a taxonomy slip is not a case
failure. Outputs were scored under opaque ids with the condition map sealed; the map was opened
only after all 18 verdicts were committed, and the blind case labels then matched the sealed
truth 18 of 18.

The honest limit: this is **condition-masked scoring, not blind scoring**. A scorer who knows
the clause can often guess the condition from an answer's shape, which is exactly why the
rubric refuses to score the appearance of the clause's own words. A single scorer also scored
every run.

## What this does not establish

- **Nothing about conduct.** A run is one turn with no tools: the subject reports on supplied
  evidence and never executes a check. These cases score reports, not behaviour while working.
- **Nothing about an ordinary session.** B is an explicitly supplied, quoted ten-row baseline.
  The result says the clause adds nothing *over that supplied text* — not that it adds nothing
  to a session carrying whatever context it really loads.
- **One model, one harness, one vendor.** `claude-opus-5` on Claude Code 2.1.271, Anthropic
  route only. A weaker model may not clear the baseline at all, and the Codex route cannot pin
  a model through the registry (C97).
- **No effect size, and a coarse instrument.** Three repeats screen for gross instability;
  pass/partial/fail cannot register a smaller difference.
- **The controls have not run.** Without the positive controls, nothing here tests whether the
  clause causes over-hedging — the failure mode it was most at risk of.
