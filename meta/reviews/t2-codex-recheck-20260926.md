## Validity, readiness, and owner decision

**Validity:** The score table is usable under its recorded scoring; the main interpretive qualification is the unresolved P2-4 E3 reading. T2 supports “no benefit detected in this sample under recorded scoring,” not equivalence or general absence of effect. The delivery comparisons remain bounded by case-project mismatch and untested native delivery.

**Readiness:** T2 is closed as a measurement and its limitations are disclosed in MAIN. The independent Codex report itself is outside the repository; its substantive findings are represented in the current audit synthesis and M84. No T2 scoring change or rerun is required by this review.

**Owner disposition:** This is distinct from T2’s validity. Current [A22 task state](../TASKS.md) records that the owner accepted no new claim-calibration rule on the combined T2/T3 evidence through ADR 0019 D01. A22 continues on other evaluation steps; C86 has no implementation scope. This review does not reopen or substitute for that recorded decision.
