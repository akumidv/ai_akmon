# A22 P3 — does the decision-ground routing clause reduce failed subject runs?

Task: A22 (the P3 V3 successor line; the packet's decisions are [ADR 0019](../../docs/adr/0019-collaboration-guidance-from-a22.md) D02–D08).

## Question

Does the frozen routing clause reduce failed subject runs? Arm C is the architect baseline B plus
only the clause; arm A, with no guidance, qualifies the packet's strata before B and C are run. The
question is a comparison of C against B, not of either against A.

## Method

- **Packet.** Eight constructed neutral cards, four P3 pairs, two tiers, two repeats. A subject run
  is two turns with no tools, from an out-of-repository scratch directory, routed by an exact menu
  id, with rollout verification after each dispatch.
- **Qualification.** Arm A runs 8 cards × 2 tiers × 2 repeats, 32 subject runs. A card and tier
  qualifies only when both blind scorers fail the same A repeat. The sealed map is fixed before B
  and C. B and C continue only with at least four qualified strata and at least one from every pair.
- **Comparison.** B and C take two repeats per qualifying stratum: at most 64 runs, at most 24 more
  for predeclared mechanics or defect reruns, 120 two-turn subject runs in all. Input is capped at
  40k tokens per turn.
- **Scoring.** Two independent blind readings per output, Codex and Qwen scorer routes, with no
  adjudication calls.
- **Predeclared success rule.** A practical benefit needs at least three fewer C failures than B
  under both scorers, and no pair with two or more extra C failures under both scorers. Any other
  outcome is inconclusive or worse, never equivalence.
- **Gate.** Before any model call the owner records the CLI, harness and model pins, the scorer
  pins, the token and spend caps and a mechanics preflight. Each attempt writes a run-attempt marker
  before dispatch, so a failed attempt consumes budget.

## Verdict

**No B/C comparison exists.** The packet ended at its qualification floor: A-only qualification
finished `FLOOR_FAILED` (M176).

- 128 of 128 primary A calls and 32 complete cells were accepted.
- Seven same-repeat runs failed under both scorers, and five strata qualified.
- Only three of the four required pairs were represented: `material-reversible` was absent. The
  rule needs at least four strata and every pair.
- [ADR 0019 D07](../../docs/adr/0019-collaboration-guidance-from-a22.md#d07--stop-the-current-p3-comparison-at-its-qualification-floor)
  stopped the packet before B and C. There is no clause-benefit result and no equivalence result.

Operational negatives, each of which cost calls or budget and none of which measures the clause:

- Qwen gateway DNS failure, `getaddrinfo EAI_AGAIN` before any model response, twice (M146, M156).
- Codex WebSocket HTTP 503 on calls that returned a reply; the frozen gate treated them as terminal
  in four calls (M167, M169, M172, M175).
- Qwen scorer format failures: prose before the TSV (M147), a truncated packet id (M155), a missing
  header (M158).
- Qwen thinking used the whole output allowance with no final text: at 2,000 tokens (M145) and at
  5,000 tokens (M165).
- CLI pin mismatch: the default `codex` was 0.162.1 where the sealed runner required 0.156.1. The
  run stopped before any provider call (M140).
- Codex quota check returned HTTP 401 `token_expired` (M163).
- Two canaries exceeded the 3,000-input post-hoc cap: 14,459 input tokens (M119) and 12,932 input
  tokens (M121).
- Charged V3 Codex use was 1,892,153 of 5,000,000 at the stop (M176).

## Limits

- The qualification ran under a sequence of successor seals after operational stops. Each successor
  changed a route or a recovery step; the rules forbid any repair that changes a card, rubric, arm or
  map, and the records state that none did.
- Five qualified strata and one missing pair give no comparison, so no effect size, direction or
  confidence statement is possible from this packet.
- Canary and mechanics runs used real packets but never entered the scored data or the qualification
  map.
- In the sealed input `seal-v3.json`, three scripts (`dispatch_v3.py`, `usage_ledger_v3.py`,
  `v3_runner.py`) differ from their recorded hashes. This predates the results document and is kept
  as found.
