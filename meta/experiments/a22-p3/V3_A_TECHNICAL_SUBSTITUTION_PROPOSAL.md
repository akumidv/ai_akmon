# A22 P3 V3 A technical-substitution proposal

The headroom A seal stopped on call 88 of 128 because the Codex scorer's CLI stderr recorded a WebSocket 503. Its response and usage exist, but its terminal outcome is failed under the sealed `OBSERVED_NO_RETRY` evidence rule. The first 87 calls were accepted, including 21 complete cells. No scorer verdict from call 88 was inspected to choose this path. The stopped seal and private runtime remain immutable.

## Owner decision proposed

Accept **one prospective technical substitution for this infrastructure-marked scorer call**, under a new seal and a new call ID. The failed call remains failed and charged. The replacement is a new, blind Codex scorer reading of the *same frozen cell-22 packet and request bytes*. It has no access to the failed answer. The first 87 accepted calls are carried forward by immutable hashes and independently replayed validators; they are never reissued or relabelled. After the replacement, run only the remaining 40 scheduled calls, preserving their original IDs, order, models, cases, prompts, rubrics, and scorer criteria. This is a one-time exception, not an automatic retry policy or a general permission to replace model-format failures.

Before any new provider call, freeze and independently review a successor source, carryover manifest, replacement call ID, schedule, per-call quota/usage gates, exact request hash, seal, default-deny approval and offline preflight. The successor must bind every accepted predecessor result and ledger hash, verify that call 88 failed specifically on the captured 503 stderr marker with correlated usage, and reject any change to its raw response or the 87 accepted outputs. It must not read or score the failed call's verdict while constructing the successor. The replacement must pass all original Codex scorer gates, including one session, exact model/version, valid TSV, correlated usage and no visible retry/error/tool marker. A failure stops the successor terminally; no second replacement is authorized.

The original A floor still needs all 32 cells, both scorers on every cell, same-repeat joint failures, at least four qualifying strata covering every case pair. A full reduction must distinguish carried-over and new readings. As a sensitivity check after the complete primary reduction, disclose whether using the failed call's otherwise parseable reply would change the floor or qualifying strata, without treating it as primary evidence. This check addresses the post-hoc replacement risk; it does not validate the failed route. No B/C comparison is authorized by this proposal.

The opening V3-only Codex charge for the successor is 1,489,919 / 5,000,000, including the failed call. Qwen has no aggregate token ceiling. Keep fresh interpretable Codex subscription telemetry below 80% five-hour and 90% weekly before each Codex call. Preserve `OBSERVED_NO_RETRY` as a claim about captured CLI evidence only. The 16,000 Qwen output setting and provider-default thinking are frozen from the successful unscored headroom route. The original ADR D02 text still names an earlier 2,000-token Qwen condition; record the owner's later accepted 16,000-token operational amendment explicitly before dispatch so the decision record matches the sealed run.

This document proposes a narrow policy change because the current seal forbids retry and repair. It authorizes no provider call. If the owner rejects carryover, the alternative is a fully fresh 128-call A qualification under a new schedule and seal; neither path may reuse call 88 as an accepted reading.
